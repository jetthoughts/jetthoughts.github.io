Quickstart · From Idea to First Paying Customer

You have an idea and no time, so this page lists only the core lessons - the smallest amount of reading that still produces a real result.

The Problem (in your language) #

You want to validate whether your idea has real demand before you spend $30K+ on a dev shop - but you can’t read code, your available time is evenings and weekends (maybe 2-4 hours a week), and hiring anyone is premature. This route works within all three constraints.

The Minimal Path (Core Lessons Only) #

Skip the lessons marked “optional” on the course landing page and you still walk away with every Founder OS artifact. Here’s the shortest route:

Module 1 - Hypothesis & Smoke Test #

Output: one-sentence hypothesis + live landing page with Stripe price button + price-test signal.

  • 1.1 · Form Your Founding Hypothesis
  • 1.2 · Smoke Test: Build the Page with an AI Builder
  • 1.3 · Wire Tracking Before Traffic Starts
  • 1.4 · Run It and Read the Signal
  • 1.5 · Price Your Hypothesis on the Smoke-Test Page

Gate: ≥6% conversion from cold traffic (the “Promising” band in Lesson 1.4’s decision table); a measured click-to-payment rate from the price test. 3-6% means iterate the message, not advance.

Module 2 - Validate the Problem #

Output: 10 Mom Test interviews + clickable prototype tested with 5 people.

  • 2.1 · The Mom Test: Ask About the Past (2.2, the optional AI interview rehearsal, is skip-safe on this fast path)
  • 2.3 · Find 10 People: Where to Look
  • 2.4 · Find 10 People: What to Say
  • 2.5 · Mom Test Synthesis: Build, Pivot, or Kill
  • 2.6 · Build a Clickable Prototype

Gate: ≥7 of 10 interviewees have spent time or money on the problem.

Module 3 - Design from Evidence #

Output: one-page Product Brief from real customer vocabulary.

  • 3.1 · The One-Page Product Brief
  • 3.2 · Quality-check: Features to Outcomes

Gate: every feature traces back to a verbatim interview quote.

Module 4 - Build It Yourself #

Output: live MVP at a real URL, ownership locked Day 1.

  • 4.1 · Should You Hire? Decision Tree
  • 4.2 · Own Your GitHub, AWS, and Database
  • 4.3 · Tools & Setup
  • 4.4 · Build Phases

Gate: you own every account; the app loads; one core flow works end-to-end. (4.5, the optional monthly ceiling check, starts after launch.)

Module 5 - First Paying Customer #

Output: one signed paid pilot + repeatable outbound channel.

  • 5.1 · Your First Customer Is Not a Marketing Problem (5.2, the optional channel-selection detour, is skip-safe on this fast path)
  • 5.3 · Build Your 50-Name Network List
  • 5.4 · Write the Outreach Message
  • 5.5 · Send, Track, and Read the Replies
  • 5.6 · Charge Before You Ship: The Paid Pilot

Gate: signed pilot agreement + deposit received through Stripe ($500+ standard; first-month-upfront on the low-price path in 5.6).
