The client

Agent Inbox is a messaging platform for real-estate agents. It uses Multiple Listing Service (MLS) listing data to bring deal-making for realtors into one place.

They wanted an MVP quickly, to attract investors and customers, and as a foundation for further development. They also wanted help building the right internal development team for the long run.

The challenge

After a full year of building the product internally, Agent Inbox still didn’t have a market-ready version, and the platform needed to be rebuilt from scratch. The internal team didn’t have enough resources or technical expertise to deliver it on time.

What we did

  • Five full-time engineers, working as Agent Inbox's development team.
  • A dedicated project advisor, so leadership could see progress as it happened.
  • A kick-off meeting to set expectations, learn the product idea and agree on a plan.
  • A simple development process that let Agent Inbox monitor its budget and get the most value from our team.
  • A team size that changed with what Agent Inbox needed at each stage.

What changed

The MVP was live in 60 days. In under a year the product added 100,000+ users. Agent Inbox’s own developer team grew from one to six, and we worked together for 50+ months.
