Before you book

How long do clients stay?

About 95% of clients stay, and the average relationship runs five years. On this page the shortest is 11+ months (OrchestrateCS) and the longest 9+ years (Mobile Coach).

How many engineers would we get?

Between one and five on these six: LimeLeads had one, Mobile Coach four, Agent Inbox five. Our engineers average 8+ years of experience.

Do you only work in Rails?

All six are Rails apps. The rest of the stack differs by client, from Hotwire to React, Vue, Node.js or Python; each case page lists it.

Will we end up dependent on you?

Our clients' own teams grew while we were there: Agent Inbox from 1 to 6 internal developers, Open Apply from 2 to 20. When you hire, we pair with your new engineers and ramp down on two weeks' notice.
