Directive (Paul, 2026-08-21): Claude Design + Stitch are collaborative
surfaces, not one-shot generators. Flow: **online research → top 2–3 design

## The finding that reframes the work

Paul's finished Claude Design artifact (project `3dc819cf-084f-4dd6-b7aa-7275c2424e68`,
"Fractional CTO.dc.html") independently converged on the SAME landing anatomy
as our unstarted 20.05 spec: problem-naming hero, month-one card,
fractional-vs-full-time decision table, free-audit-first timeline, case cards,
named verified testimonial, repeated CTA. External research (below) converges
on it too.

**Conclusion: the anatomy is settled. The variable worth piloting is the
REGISTER** (palette + type + surface language). All pilots share one skeleton;
each wears a different skin. That makes the lightning demo a controlled
comparison instead of apples-to-oranges.

## Research notes (what the sweep supports)

- **Trust cues are the differentiator for skeptical B2B buyers**: third-party
  review ratings, named quotes with real title/company, quantified outcomes,
  case studies — placed above the fold and near decision points, not in a
  basement section. Buyers protect their own career; sloppy design reads as
  risk. (trajectorywebdesign, userevidence, windmillstrategy, bopdesign)
- **More information converts for complex/high-ticket B2B** — comparison
  tables, FAQs, process transparency; CTAs repeat at multiple scroll points;
  forms ≤5 fields. (genpage, unicornplatform, perspective)
- **2026 register split**: (a) *editorial text-first* — typography as the
  interface, serif boldness, page-weight discipline; (b) *engineered
  precision* — sharp geometry, 1px borders, stark neo-grotesque, "premium
  brutalism". Both are anti-template signals; decoration is what reads
  template. (designmonks, setproduct, fireart, brainy.ink)
- **Peer register (thoughtbot.com)**: spacious dark-first sans, quantified
  proof ("1,000+ positive outcomes"), conversational CTAs ("Let's talk"),
  generous whitespace = confidence. Validates restraint-over-decoration for
  dev consultancies.

## Pilot mechanics

One stub per candidate on the rail: `/next/pilots/<register>/fractional-cto/`
(cascade noindex covers all of `/next/`), each with its own CSS bundle —
the rail's per-page CSS ownership makes a 3-way skin comparison ~free.
Lightning demo = side-by-side links + screenshots + 3-line verdicts per lens
(5-second trust, CTA clarity, mobile), then discussion; Claude Design and
Stitch remain the iteration surfaces for the chosen register.
