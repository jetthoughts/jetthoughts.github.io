## 5. News policy — durable vs event (the flexibility question, answered)

The plan should be reactive to *some* news and rigidly closed to the rest. The
test is one question:

> **Will someone search this in six months?**

| Type | Example | Home | Slots |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Durable news** - CVEs, releases, EOLs, new defaults | `CVE-2026-66066`, "rails 7 eol" | Blog, fast | **Swap**, never add |
| **Event news** - incidents, drama, funding | PocketOS DB wipe | LinkedIn only | Zero blog slots |

Evidence this is the right cut: the PocketOS post scored 48/50, ran the full
9-persona pipeline, and produced **1 click in 3 months**. Nobody searches an
incident twice. A CVE ID is a permanent search string with multi-year audit-lag
traffic.

**Mechanism - a swap rule, not a new lane.** A durable-news item may *displace*
the lowest-value queued row; the displaced row returns to the queue. Net
posts/week is unchanged, deliberately, because throughput is the binding
constraint. Adding a reactive lane to a plan already behind just grows the
backlog.

**Refresh debt is the hidden cost.** Version-current content decays. We already
have the proof: the Kamal 1/Traefik post is wrong and still ranking. Every
version-pinned post (including the new E4, pinned to Kamal 2.12.0) incurs a
refresh obligation. **Budget 1 refresh slot per month** or this compounds.

## 6. P3 — The one post to write this week

**CVE-2026-66066 "KindaRails2Shell"** - Active Storage arbitrary file read → RCE,
CVSS 9.5, disclosed Jul 29, patched in 7.2.3.2 / 8.0.5.1 / 8.1.3.1.

Why this one clears the bar when almost nothing else does:

- **Durable**: CVE IDs are permanent search strings.
- **In our authority zone**: Rails technical is the only content earning clicks.
- **Underserved angle**: most coverage says "upgrade Rails." That is **not
  sufficient** - the bug is in how Active Storage hands uploads to libvips, so
  libvips must go to ≥8.13, *and* `secret_key_base`, DB credentials, and storage
  keys must be rotated because an arbitrary file read may already have taken
  them. The rotation step is what teams miss.
- **Converts through security, not through the crowded category keyword.**
- **Rails ≤7.1 has no patch** (security support ended 2025-10-01), which turns
  row N3 into a live-exposure post rather than a housekeeping one.

**Shipped 2026-08-08** as `rails-cve-2026-66066-active-storage-rce` (PR #439).
The review loop caught four blockers, one of which inverted the post's thesis:
the draft said the patched gem silently no-ops on old libvips, but the advisory
says Active Storage raises at boot. As drafted the runbook would have taken a
reader's production down on Ubuntu 22.04. Runbook now puts libvips first.

Sources: [Rapid7](https://www.rapid7.com/blog/post/etr-kindarails2shell-cve-2026-66066-critical-arbitrary-file-read-and-possible-remote-code-execution-in-ruby-on-rails/) · [Akamai](https://www.akamai.com/blog/security-research/rails-active-storage-rce-cve-2026-66066) · [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/rails-patches-critical-active-storage-flaw-with-rce-potential/) · [HeroDevs](https://www.herodevs.com/blog-posts/cve-2026-66066-rails-active-storage-arbitrary-file-read-and-rce)

## 7. Revised stream weighting (Aug 7 → Nov 30)

At 6 posts/month, against a Nov 30 deadline:

| Stream | Share | Purpose | Ranking matters? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Stream 0 — LinkedIn** | 3-4/wk | The only channel with days-scale feedback before Nov 30 | No |
| **Sales enablement** | ~2/mo | Artifacts to *send a prospect*: audit scorecard, ownership checklist, rebuild-vs-refactor tool | **No - this is the point** |
| **Rails technical** | ~2/mo | Authority maintenance; upgrade-in-place and consolidation first | Yes |
| **Durable news** | swap-in | CVEs, EOLs, releases | Yes |
| **Refresh** | 1/mo | Pay down version-decay debt | Protects existing |

**The reframe that resolves the 20.08 ↔ 2607 conflict**: a post can serve sales
enablement without ever ranking. "Something Paul can send a prospect mid-call" is
a legitimate, measurable deliverable that 20.08 had no category for. Judging
those posts by clicks is why founder content looked worthless.

### Time-horizon honesty

SEO on a new cluster takes 6-12 months; 20.08 says so itself. The deadline is
under 4 months. **Phase 3 "New Territory" (N1-N10) cannot pay off before Nov 30**
- it is a 2027 investment scheduled straight through the October convert window.
Defer it.
