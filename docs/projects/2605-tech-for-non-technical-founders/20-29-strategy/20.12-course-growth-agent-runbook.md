## Standing decisions (Paul, 2026-07-30 - do not relitigate without new pilot data)

1. **Fully ungated, NO mail list.** Every lesson, template, and PDF stays a
   direct link. No email forms, no ESP, no gating. The "No sign-up" landing
   chip is a deliberate trust position for the stealth-stage ICP.
2. **Clarity waived - GA4 is the whole measurement stack.** Stall diagnosis =
   GA4 funnel drop-offs + pilot-founder debriefs. No session recordings.
3. **No selling inside the course** (Option C pure lead magnet). Zero service
   CTAs. Footer authorship credit only. The reader->JT-lead bridge is
   DEFERRED until after the pilot.
4. **No public/social share mechanics** (stealth ICP - idea-stage founders
   don't broadcast that they're validating). Private 1:1 sharing only.
5. **Distribution CAMPAIGNS are pilot-gated** (blog funnel pushes, LinkedIn
   campaign). Internal linking and SEO hygiene are NOT campaigns and are
   executable now.

### G2.1 Blog -> course internal links (the 0-of-580 bug)

- **Why**: 0 of ~580 blog posts link to the course; internal linking is SEO
  hygiene, independent of the pilot gate.
- **Tools**: rg, Edit, chrome-devtools for scroll gate.
- **Steps**: (1) shortlist 8-12 EVERGREEN posts by topic match - rg blog
  content for: idea validation, MVP, first customers / first paying, hiring
  a developer / dev shop, non-technical founder, landing page test, customer
  interviews. Prefer posts already ranking (check GA4 top blog pages if
  accessible, else topical judgment). (2) Add ONE short house-style callout
  per post linking the MATCHING module/lesson (not always the landing page -
  deep-link 2.1 for interview posts, 4.1 for hiring posts, etc.).
  (3) Callout copy must pass voice gates; no two posts may reuse the same
  callout sentence (rotation rule).
- **DoD**: 8-12 posts each carry one callout; scroll gate screenshots per
  post; `bin/rake test:critical` green; one PR.

### G2.2 SERP + metadata spot-check

- **Tools**: browser (search engine), Edit.
- **Steps**: search the course's target phrases from the landing/lesson
  keywords (e.g. "non technical founder course", "validate startup idea
  course free", "idea to first paying customer"). Record position +
  which page appears + how the snippet reads. Fix ONLY metadata gaps found
  (missing/weak description, truncated title). No keyword stuffing, no
  new content.
- **DoD**: a SERP-snapshot table appended to this runbook (query, position,
  page, snippet issue, fix applied); metadata edits in the same G2 PR.

### G2.3 Private referral affordance

- **Tools**: theme templates + JS; chrome-devtools for click test.
- **Steps**: one "Copy link to send a founder friend" button (clipboard API,
  no social icons, no share URLs with trackers) on the course landing +

  (`data-course-event="copy-share-link"`-equivalent) on click.
- **DoD**: button matches house style; click copies the canonical URL and

**P0 MEASUREMENT FINDING (needs Paul decision):** every collect hit carried
`gcs=G100` (consent DENIED) and returned HTTP 503. Cause:
`privacyCompliant = true` in config/_default/hugo.toml:91 sets
`gtag('consent','default',{analytics_storage:'denied'})` for ALL visitors,
and NO consent banner exists anywhere to ever grant it - GA4 runs
permanently in cookieless-ping mode. The 503s may additionally be
local-network/extension noise, but even at best GA4 receives only modeled
pings, never full sessions. **The pilot's "GA4-only" measurement stack is
effectively muted as built.** Options for Paul: (a) add a
privacy-preserving consent banner (deny default, one-click accept) so
consenting visitors produce real sessions; (b) verify in the GA4 property
whether consent-mode modeling shows ANY course data since #356 shipped -
if yes, accept modeled-only; (c) reconsider the analytics approach.
Agents must NOT change the privacy posture unilaterally.
