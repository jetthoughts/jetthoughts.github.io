# 20.13 - Reader -> JT-Lead Bridge: Design Options (Paul's decision)

**Date**: 2026-07-31
**Status**: PROPOSAL - build NOTHING until Paul picks an option (Wave H gate;
this touches Standing Decision 3 in 20.12: no selling inside the course).
**Context**: pilot gate removed 2026-07-31; the bridge was the last deferred
item without a design. Today the only bridge is the footer authorship credit.

## What the bridge must respect

- The course's core trust position: free, ungated, no sign-up, zero service
  CTAs. Sam picked this course BECAUSE it doesn't sell to him.
- Sam (idea-stage, stealth) is not a JT lead today - he becomes one 6-18
  months later, IF his validation succeeds and he needs to hire or rescue.
  The bridge's job is to be findable at that later moment, not to convert now.
- Measurement exists: GA4 course funnel + Clarity are live, so any bridge
  click is attributable.

## Option 1 - Footer authorship expansion (inside course, quietest)

One line added to the existing per-lesson footer credit: who JetThoughts is
plus what the team does, linking /services/ once.
- **Trust cost**: low but nonzero - the credit line becomes a pitch line on
  EVERY lesson; repeat exposure reads as advertising by lesson 10.
- **Yield**: low; footers are banner-blind zones.
- **Effort**: one partial edit + both visual suites.

## Option 2 - Journey-end "when you hire help" note (inside course, contextual)

A single short paragraph at the natural moments where hiring help becomes
real: the 5.7 course outro (and possibly the Module 4 hiring-track close).
Framed as course content ("when you reach the point of hiring a team, here
is what to check - the authors do this for a living"), one link.
- **Trust cost**: medium - it IS a service mention inside course body, the
  exact thing Standing Decision 3 bans. Mitigation: it appears only after
  the reader has extracted the full value (course end), where "who wrote
  this and can they help" is a question Sam actually has.
- **Yield**: medium - readers who finish are the highest-intent slice.
- **Effort**: 1-2 content edits + Sam-voice review; GA4 event on the link.

## Option 3 - Off-course bridge (course stays 100% clean) - RECOMMENDED

The course body changes NOT AT ALL. The bridge lives on the surfaces around
it: the Wave G blog-funnel posts link both INTO the course (education) and,
where genuinely relevant, to the matching JT service (the posts are marketing
surfaces where the 90/10 rule already permits it); /services/ and use-case
pages cite the course as proof of expertise ("we wrote the free course on
this"). Sam's later hiring moment finds JT through the same content that
brought him to the course.
- **Trust cost**: ZERO inside the course - Standing Decision 3 stays intact
  verbatim.
- **Yield**: compounding - every Wave G post is a bridge; services pages get
  a credibility artifact.
- **Effort**: rides Wave G execution (the briefs' internal-link plans) plus
  ~3 service-page one-liners. No course edits, no visual suites.

## Recommendation

Ship **Option 3 now** (it is nearly free - it rides Wave G) and hold
**Option 2 as the candidate follow-up** once course-completion data (GA4
5.x page_views) shows a meaningful finisher cohort worth bridging. Option 1
is dominated by both and proposed only for completeness.

**Decision needed from Paul**: pick 1 / 2 / 3 / 3-then-2 (recommended) / none.
