# Kill-criterion panel - 2026-08-20

**Decision made autonomously** under the manager autonomy grant (Paul, reinforced
2026-08-20: "instead of involving me run brainstorm voting and make an own call").
Recorded here with its evidence so Paul can audit and reverse. The criterion
itself lives in `docs/workflows/linkedin-icp-validation-plan.md` § "Kill
criterion" - this file is the reasoning, not a second copy.

## The problem

`linkedin-posts/metrics-ledger.md` asserted: *"Each lane's plan has a 2-week kill
criterion."* Neither plan had one. Grep found the only "kill" strings in either
lane plan to be `linkedin-course-promo-plan.md:16` (a promise made inside course
content about a founder's own product idea, not a campaign gate) and `:80`, a
post hook. What the plans carry is a four-item **Decision rules** list that
steers - which pillar to lean into, how to re-angle a hook - and never stops
anything.

Consequence: the campaign could not fail, only continue. The sprint's stated
premise ("the kill criteria are untestable because the ledger is empty") was
itself wrong - they were never written.

## Panel

Three independent lenses, briefed separately with the same verified numbers, no
cross-talk before reporting.

| Lens | Core position |
|---|---|
| **Growth / distribution** | Absolute criterion, campaign-level, 8 posts, 3 replies, 3,000-impression gate, arrival *override* tightened to campaign UTM |
| **Lean / experiment design** | Post-count unit, **10** posts (rule of three), 3 replies, strike the old targets, add a non-execution trigger |
| **ICP / voice of customer** | Criterion must be **comparative** (LinkedIn vs an ICP-native pond), 2-of-6, arrival *veto*, and `icp_replies` is not currently countable |

## Unanimous (adopted without modification)

1. **Post-count unit, never a calendar window.** All three, independently. A
   time-boxed window at this cadence measures Paul's availability, not the market.
2. **The window has not started until rows are filled.** ICP proposed it, lean
   adopted it verbatim as the best clause in either proposal, growth's
   non-execution clause is compatible. It blocks the most likely real failure:
   a verdict declared off an empty table.
3. **Threshold of 3 `icp_replies`.** Growth and lean landed on 3 independently,
   both anchoring it to the same existing repo rule - the plan's ICP Update Rules
   already require a signal to appear 3 times. Kill at <3, update the ICP at >=3:
   one ladder, not two unrelated numbers. One reply is indistinguishable from a
   friendly ex-colleague.
4. **A reach guard, so a low-reach zero cannot condemn the message.** All three.
5. **This kills the channel, never the ICP.** All three, emphatically. Never edit
   `90.10` on a LinkedIn zero.
6. **The old Measurement targets cannot be a gate.** All three. Struck to
   "aspirations" - an impossible target left in a doc gets quoted by a cold
   session as evidence of failure.
7. **Audience composition is the dominant confound.** All three.

## Split decisions - my calls

### 1. n = 10 for the kill, 6 for a mandatory no-kill review

ICP said 6, growth said 8, lean said 10. **Lean wins on evidence**, and it is the
only lens that showed its arithmetic: by the rule of three, zero events in n
gives p < 3/n at 95%, so n=6 with zero replies only excludes "more than half of
posts produce a reply" - you cannot retire a channel on that. n=10 excludes
p>26%, i.e. "fewer than one qualified conversation a month at this cadence",
which is a defensible stop. Growth's 8 split the difference without the same
grounding.

ICP's 6 is not discarded - it becomes the **mandatory review point** with no kill
authority, which is where lean's leading indicator lives. Each n gets the
decision it can actually carry.

**Per lane, not campaign-level** (contra growth): the two lanes address
different people - course lane targets Sam the idea-stage first-timer, rescue
lane targets Alex the burned founder. Pooling would let course-lane replies mask
rescue-lane silence, which is precisely the conflation the criterion exists to
prevent. Growth's throughput objection is real and is answered by the
non-execution trigger, which *is* correctly per-lane and can fire in 8 weeks.

### 2. Absolute criterion, with the comparison as the kill ACTION (contra ICP)

The ICP lens made the strongest single evidentiary contribution - that LinkedIn
is a small minority of where this ICP actually speaks - and I verified it
directly rather than inheriting it. **Correcting their numbers**: they reported
27 harvested quotes with 2 from LinkedIn; the actual harvest in
`voice-of-customer.md` is 14 IndieHackers / 13 Reddit / **3 LinkedIn** / 1 X.
The proportion claim survives and is the important part - LinkedIn is roughly a
tenth of the founder voice we could find.

But growth's rebuttal to making the *criterion* comparative is decisive on three
counts, and I accept it:

- **The comparison arm has the same execution disease as the arm under test.**
  3 of 16 LinkedIn drafts published; *zero* Reddit/IH posts published. The
  two-ponds policy is already declared in `linkedin-posts/README.md` and has
  produced nothing. You cannot gate a decision on a comparator that is not running.
- **One publisher cannot power two arms.** Splitting 2-3 posts/week across two
  ponds gives ~4 and ~4 - comparing two under-powered arms is worse than
  measuring one properly, for double the drafting load, at the exact moment
  throughput is the binding constraint.
- **Baking the historical ~10% in pre-decides H5**, which is precisely the claim
  that targeted reply-CTAs change that rate.

The comparison survives **sequentially, as the kill action**: on a reach kill the
same hooks move to Reddit/IH unchanged. Same information, no parallel drafting
load, and it runs only when it is the live question.

### 3. Arrival override, not veto (contra ICP)

ICP proposed that any LinkedIn-attributed arrival blocks a kill. Rejected on
growth's argument, which I find airtight: there were ~4 LinkedIn-attributed
arrivals in 28 days with **one post ever published** - those are baseline
(profile link, bio, DMs), not campaign output. A veto firing on baseline makes
the campaign unkillable again, *which is the exact bug being fixed*.

Two further disqualifiers: channel attribution is too loose (50.02 documented
~93% Direct because LinkedIn strips referrers), and per the link policy only the
course lane puts a UTM'd link in the first comment - so a channel-attribution
veto would give the course lane a permanent free pass and could never fire for
the rescue lane.

Adopted instead in growth's tightened form: **>= 2 sessions carrying
`utm_campaign=icp_validation_*`** overrides a kill. The campaign UTM isolates
campaign traffic from baseline; the floor of 2 guards against Paul or a
teammate clicking his own link. ICP's underlying point - an arrival is closer to
the business goal than a reply - is preserved as an override rather than a veto.

## Shipped alongside, from panel findings (already committed)

- **`icp_replies` was not countable.** Defined as "count the ones using ICP
  language" plus five examples; two readers of the same six-comment thread could
  land anywhere from 0 to 5. Replaced with a three-clause test (SELF + ARTIFACT
  + NON-SUPPLIER), one human counted once, and a rule for near-misses: ask the
  clarifying question, count the answer.
- **The ledger could not record the plan's own primary metrics.** The plan names
  Qualified DMs and ICP-role profile views as headline metrics; the ledger had no
  `dms` column at all. Added `dms`, `icp_profile_views`, `reply_protocol_run`.

## Known weaknesses (stated, not hidden)

- **3,000 impressions is provisional.** It is the point where observing zero
  becomes genuinely surprising at an assumed ~0.1% substantive-reply rate.
  Recalibrate from Paul's real median once three rows are logged.
- **The validity gate is imperfect.** 3,000 impressions of an engineer/agency
  audience reads identically to 3,000 of the right people, which is why the
  viewer-title check sits above the branches rather than in a footnote.
- **The arrival override is a stretch.** 10 posts x ~400 impressions x a 1-2%
  first-comment click rate is a handful of clicks. That is why it is an override
  and not a floor: if it fires, it is real.
- **The binding constraint today is not the criterion.** It is that 16 drafts
  exist, 3 are out, and zero are logged. A criterion evaluated against an empty
  ledger fires for reasons unrelated to the ICP. The non-execution trigger is the
  clause that names this honestly.
