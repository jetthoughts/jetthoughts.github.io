## 1. CONVERGENT findings (the punch-list)

### P1. Relocate "Going further (after first paying customer)" body off the landing page - 4/4 lenses
- **Move:** relocate the three trigger tables (L192-215); keep the H2, one Sam-voice sentence, and a link on the landing page.
- **Target surface (real, existing):** `first-paying-customer-operating-kit/` - already framed as the post-Module-5 kit - with the `founder-os-pack/` index as fallback. No new page needed.
- **Rationale (combined):** content whose stated trigger is "you passed the Module 5 gate" sits on the page that recruits people who haven't started Lesson 1.1 (IA). For Sam it previews churn, org charts, and AI token bills before lesson one - anxiety, not motivation. Cold-eyes: "exactly where I stopped reading." Conversion: zero contribution to the 1.1 click, pushes two acquisition sections below 89% depth.
- **Why keep the H2 stub:** the anchor `#going-further-after-first-paying-customer` has 5 inbound links (see Risks). A one-sentence stub preserves them and keeps the "course continues past customer one" completeness signal Sam values.

### P2. Promote "Already started building?" as a short router; move the checklist body out - 4/4 lenses
- **Move:** place a 1-2 line segment router adjacent to "Take this course if / Skip if" ("Already paying someone to build? Start here instead"). Keep the H2 (anchor `#already-started-building` has an inbound link from `how-this-course-works/`), condensed to the fork itself.
- **Target for the 6-step checklist body:** the Ownership Audit lesson page `github-aws-database-ownership-checklist/` (step 1's own destination) or a dedicated side-path page under the course - checklist must land on a real surface before it leaves the landing page.
- **Rationale (combined):** all four lenses agree this is acquisition content for a second segment (mid-build founders - arguably the JT ICP) placed where that segment never reaches: 89% scroll depth. They self-disqualify at "first-time founder with an idea" in the hero and bounce. Routing belongs where entry decisions happen - first 30% of the page.

### P3. Promote "Who built this" above the module map - 4/4 lenses
- **Move:** relocate the 32-word block (L234-236) to sit adjacent to "Why this course exists" / the qualification block. Keep it short; position is the defect, not length.
- **Rationale (combined):** "who are these people and why is this free" is a pre-decision input - it's asked before reading 1,064 words of syllabus, not after (IA, conversion). "Why this course exists" already leans on "since 2011" with no anchor (cold-eyes). Free course from a dev shop makes Sam hunt for the catch; the trust anchor is parked at 100% scroll where it decides nothing.

### P4. Cut the share line ("send it to a founder friend") - 4/4 lenses (3 cut, 1 relocate - same outcome for this page)
- **Move:** delete L230-232 from the landing page. Optionally re-home the line on a completion surface (`first-paying-customer-operating-kit/` close, or Module 5.6/5.7 "Next" blocks).
- **Rationale (combined):** a referral ask presumes delivered value - a completion behavior. Phrased in past tense ("saved you") to a reader who hasn't started, it is inert (IA), noise (Sam), an orphaned line (cold-eyes), and it occupies the most valuable slot on the page - the last thing a fully-scrolled reader sees (conversion).

### P5. Add a closing "Start Lesson 1.1 →" CTA as the final element - 2/4 lenses (conversion explicit; IA names the identical gap)
- **Move:** repeat the hero primary CTA at the end of the page. After P1-P4 the page ends: module map → (moved-up sections gone) → stub sections → CTA.
- **Rationale (combined):** the only start link is in the hero; the reader at maximum conviction (bottom of the syllabus) has no action and currently exits into tables and a bio. IA: "the last actionable start-the-course link is at the top." Zero canon risk - it is the same free, ungated lesson link.

### P6. Relocate the "Optional reference" deep-dive rows out of the module cards - 3/4 lenses (IA, Sam, cold-eyes; conversion goes further - see D3)
- **Move:** remove the `Optional reference:` extras rows (~15-20 links across the 5 cards, L87, L108, L125, L146, L168) from the landing page. Each deep-dive link must already be (or become) linked from the lesson it extends or from `reference/` - verify before deleting (see Risks).
- **Keep:** `Templates:` rows (Sam: the promised artifacts are the sell), `See it in action:` walkthrough rows, chapter lists, skip-notes on 2.2/4.5/5.2/5.7 (cold-eyes: they shrink perceived workload).
- **Rationale (combined):** the rows serve someone already inside the course, not a decider (IA). Pre-commit, Sam can't distinguish required from optional volume, so the link wall inflates the perceived commitment. Cold-eyes skipped all of them on first visit.

### P7. Trim the module-map intro paragraph (L72) - 2/4 lenses (Sam, cold-eyes)
- **Move:** split the 70+ word run-on into two short sentences; cut the "(and if you ever raise, the same folder doubles as your evidence pack)" clause. Sam: fundraising language before Lesson 1.1 is a stakes-raiser, and the page itself declares fundraising out of scope (L180) - an internal contradiction.
- **Divergence inside the finding:** Sam would also move the Founder OS pack link to end-of-Module-5; cold-eyes says the printable-cover-sheet link can stay. **Resolution:** keep the link (one link is cheap; the pack index is a real orientation surface), cut only the fundraising clause and the sentence sprawl. Surgical.

### P8. Reduce the top navigation cluster to two doors - 2/4 lenses agree on "reduce" (cold-eyes merge, conversion cut; IA says keep)
- **Move:** compress L44-46 into one line with at most two choices: start (already in hero) vs. "See the full syllabus ↓". Keep the How This Course Works + FAQ links but demote them into that single line or below the qualification block - do NOT delete them (canon: `how-this-course-works` is the course's own orientation surface and links back into this page).
- **Rationale:** five destinations before any content (cold-eyes: "I don't know which door to open"); two exit links in the CTA blast radius aimed at the hesitant reader (conversion). IA's "routing device is correct" survives - the routing stays, the door count drops.

## 2. DIVERGENT findings (one lens, or lenses disagree)

### D1. "What this course does NOT cover" - 2v2 split
- IA: merge upward into "Take this course if" (qualification asked once, early). Conversion: relocate to FAQ, leave a pointer. Sam and cold-eyes: keep as-is - it lowers anxiety and is the page's cheapest trust builder ("a course that lists its own exclusions has nothing to hide").
- **Recommendation: move it up, unmerged - place the section directly after "Take this course if / Skip if", before the module map.** This satisfies IA's single-qualification-moment argument and conversion's "drag after the sell" complaint while preserving the full anxiety-lowering list Sam and cold-eyes value. Do not bury it in the FAQ: for a free product from a dev shop, visible anti-scope IS the anti-upsell proof, and canon (zero service CTAs, nothing to hide) argues for keeping it on-page. Note: the section's "(in Going Further)" reference (L181) must be re-pointed after P1.

### D2. Hero total-time number - Sam only
- Sam wants one chip or lede clause with total end-to-end time ("2-3 weeks to first checkpoint" never adds up to a total; can't commit to an unbounded thing).
- **Recommendation: adopt, gated on canon.** Add a total only if a canonical end-to-end number exists (check `.okf/content/` canonical numbers and `how-this-course-works` time budgets). Do NOT invent "6-10 weeks" - fabricating a cohort stat is a banned pattern. If no canon number exists, escalate to Paul as a one-line question rather than shipping a guess.

### D3. Compress Modules 2-5 cards to badge + Output + "You leave with" - conversion only
- Conversion argues chapter lists on cards 2-5 serve browsers, not deciders, and ~30 deep links are exit ramps. But Sam ("chapter lists make the route feel concrete and finite - I can count the lessons") and cold-eyes ("the product and the reason I scrolled; I trust the pattern") both name the chapter lists as the sell.
- **Recommendation: reject compression; ship P6 only.** Removing the Optional-reference rows already takes out most of the link wall. Gutting chapter lists trades the ICP's strongest reassurance for a hypothetical click-through gain - wrong trade for a free, ungated course whose "conversion" is trust, not urgency.

### D4. Mid-page CTA after the Module 1 card - conversion only
- **Recommendation: skip for now.** The closing CTA (P5) covers the conviction moment; a mid-syllabus CTA interrupts the map read Sam and cold-eyes praised. Revisit only if scroll analytics later show mass exit after Module 1's card.

### D5. Templates rows relocation - IA only (Sam explicitly keeps them)
- **Recommendation: keep templates rows on the cards.** 2/4 explicitly value them as the promised-artifact proof; IA is alone.

### D6. "What's in it for JetThoughts" sentence - Sam only
- Sam wants one honest sentence naming the catch (why a dev shop gives this away).
- **Recommendation: adopt inside P3, canon-checked.** One plain sentence in "Who built this" (e.g., the existing "what we wish every founder had on their desk" line already carries most of this) - no service pitch, no link to services, zero service-CTA canon holds. Wordsmith at implementation; do not add a second paragraph.
