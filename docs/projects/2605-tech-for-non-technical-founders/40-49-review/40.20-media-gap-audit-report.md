## 6. Pilot recommendation

Per 10.06 §3, the highest-leverage assets to create first:

1. **P0 — Pre-launch checklist (M4)** — 10 min, fills a real operational gap
2. **P0 — Interview scorecard (M2)** — 20 min, replaces "score in your head"
3. **P1 — Outreach cadence SVG (`outreach-sequence-template`)** — 30 min,
   adds visual hook to a text-only template

These three are the pilot. They test the recommendation workflow end-to-end:
identify gap → design asset → create → verify against brand tokens → commit.

**Pilot executed 2026-07-26.** Shipped: `interview-scorecard` page (+ SVG +
cover), `pre-launch-checklist` page (+ SVG + cover), `channel-fit-canvas.svg`
on the channel-selection lesson, and 3 SVGs on `outreach-sequence-template`.
Both new pages are linked from the course `_index.md` template lists and their
companion lessons (2.1 and 4.4). Deferred: fillable PDF exports, all non-P0
assets in §4, and the §5 interaction-pattern fixes.
