# 40.26 — Landing L5-condense spec (Paul's density directive, 2026-08-01)

Paul's directive (2026-08-01, supersedes 40.25's "all copy is contracted by
40.24" line): the landing page has ONE job — attract the idea-stage
non-technical founder and get them to click "Start Lesson 1.1". The shuffle
reference demos (tmp/shuffle-{1,2,3}-full.jpeg; demo 1 is his top pick) carry
far less on-screen content than our page; that lower density is the target.
Native `<details><summary>` expanders (no JS) are explicitly allowed for
secondary content — this un-kills 40.25's "modules collapse" item, which was
rejected only because it was assumed to need JS. Anything deeper than an
expander moves to companion pages (how-this-course-works is the full-syllabus
companion and already re-shows every module's lesson table).

## Moves (executed on branch `course-landing-l3`, on top of the L4 commits)

1. **Module cards → compact + expander.** Visible per card: module badge,
   title, the Output line (glosses kept — first-mention rule), a ONE-line
   "You leave with", a "Start Module N →" first-lesson link, and the Mia
   walkthrough row (PROTECTED — strongest converter). The full lesson list,
   the "Start here if" router line, and the template/glossary rows move
   INSIDE a native `<details><summary>N lessons</summary>` per card — still
   on-page for SEO and link equity, closed by default. course-list.css: the
   V2.3 two-col grid (chapters left / leave right) is deleted — with lessons
   collapsed there is no second column to balance, which also deletes the
   module-4/5 void workaround it required.
2. **NOT-cover → compact.** Intro sentence + the seven exclusions as a tight
   two-column list, each item one short clause. The longer why-excluded
   explanations (with their outbound links) move into ONE
   `<details><summary>Why these are out</summary>` under the list. FAQ
   checked 2026-08-01: it does not carry these explanations, so no
   duplication either way.
3. **Prose diet.** Paragraphs not doing attract/qualify/route work are cut or
   halved: the two post-intro router lines merge into one; the module-map
   preamble loses the Founder-OS elaboration (link kept); the
   Already-started-building stub body merges to two sentences. KEPT intact:
   hero + Free line, the 2-3-week checkpoint sentence, Take/Skip-if (YC link
   stays in Skip-if), the 95-word Who-built block, the mistake cards
   (5 + CTA cell), both stub H2s byte-identical, the endcap.
