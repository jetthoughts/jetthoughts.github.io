Every time I see a company with a dedicated QA department, I question their ability to deliver effectively.

It's not that QA specialists are ineffective; rather, the lengthy handoffs, toil work and slow feedback lead to missing deadlines which lead to stress and blames, which are the biggest wastes.

This responsibility lies with the executives who lack trust in their people and create hierarchies, expecting that low-morale employees will perform only if pushed by another low-morale manager.
