Plenty of struggling teams are waiting for someone to give them permission to stop struggling.

They know what's broken. They've known for weeks. But they sit with it, hoping someone above them says "yes, go fix that" - as if the pain isn't reason enough.

You don't need a yes to solve your own problem. If something slows you down every day, that's the permission.

This is the whole point of a retrospective. It isn't a ritual to report status. It's where you spot the next thing that's hurting you and go fix it yourself - no one's approval required.
