# Trends analysis — fuel for curation / reaction posts

**Why:** the two pillars we do zero of (curation/reaction, build-in-public) need
outside material. This is the input that keeps the feed part of the conversation
instead of a broadcast. Refresh the scan roughly monthly (or when a big shift
lands), turn each trend into a post angle with a clear pillar and a JT point of
view - never just "here's a link", always Paul's take.

## How to scan (repeatable)

1. Web-search the ICP's world: non-technical founders + AI/no-code building
   (Lovable, v0, bolt.new, Cursor, Blink, Base44), the vibe-coding debate,
   dev-shop economics, MVP validation. Watch what *founders* are arguing about.
2. For each live trend, write: the trend (1 line, with a source), JT's POV, the
   pillar, and whether it earns a course link (usually NOT - reaction/teach are
   the free 70%).
3. Drop the best 2-3 into `content-plan.md` for the next window.

## Guardrails

- Every reaction post carries JT's POV, not just a summary or a link (algorithm
  penalizes low-effort; voice guide bans empty curation).
- Cite the trend honestly; don't repeat unverifiable stats as fact (e.g. the
  "92%/0.02%" vibe-coding numbers are unverified - say so if used).
- Reaction/teach posts are the free 70% - resist adding a course link.
