# LinkedIn feed strategy — from promo machine to worth-following (PROPOSAL 2026-08-13)

**Status:** proposal, awaiting Paul's call before we restructure `content-plan.md`.
**Trigger:** Paul — "our feed shouldn't be just a sales/promotion machine; we
should be interesting to follow."

## The diagnosis (be honest)

Our LI OS today = two lanes (`course-promo`, `rescue-validation`). **Both are
funnels.** Nearly every post carries a course link in the first comment or exists
to test the ICP. By the standard 80/20 test - *"would someone benefit from this
post even if they never buy anything from you?"* - we pass on the *body* (the
posts give real tactics) but the *system* is 100% ask. That's the promo-machine
feeling. Well-written asks are still asks.

## What the creators Paul rates actually do

- **Luca Rossi (Refactoring, 170k+):** one teaching essay a week, illustrated in
  the exact house style we now copy. The newsletter is the product; the feed
  gives the ideas away.
- **John Cutler (132k):** prolific product-thinking frameworks, sketches, and
  open questions. Frameworks are free; trust is the asset.
- **Allen Holub (75k):** contrarian one-liners / hot takes with an image, then
  *lives in the comments*. No CTA. "LinkedIn has the most lively discussions."

Pattern: **give the value away with no ask, hold a clear point of view, show up
consistently, and argue in the comments.** The sale is reputation.

## What the 2026 data says (converges hard)

- **Content mix for founders: ~70% value / 20% personal-story / 10% promo**
  (LinkedInRank, LaGrowthMachine, Co.Actor all land 70-20-10 or 80/20). Flip it
  and people hide/unfollow.
- **Promotion with integrity:** frame the promo as a story/lesson (the customer
  problem, the trade-offs, the result) so it's valuable even un-clicked. Our
  exhibits + the $40k post already do this - that's the *10%*, not the whole feed.
- **Engagement slot ~1/week:** polls (206% more reach), open questions, hot takes.
  Best place to test a new topic. We have polls ✓.
- **Curation (4-1-1 rule):** share/react to *others'* content. **We do zero of
  this** - it's the cheapest way to be in the conversation instead of broadcasting.
- **Format shift:** single images now underperform plain text by ~30%; carousels
  (3-4 images / 6-9 slides) get up to 596% more engagement. Our single exhibits
  are strong but a multi-point one could ship as a 3-4 slide carousel for reach.
- **Timing:** Tue-Thu, ~10-11am ET - our 15:00-17:00 CEST already hits this ✓.
- Founder account > company page (561% more reach) ✓ we're already personal.
- Posts about **mistakes/changed-my-mind** outperform success stories.

## The proposal: classify by VALUE PILLAR, enforce 70-20-10

Keep course/rescue as *topics*, but tag every post by what it *gives*, and hold
the ratio across each ~2-week window:

| Share | Pillar | What it is | Course link? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **70% value** | **Teach** | a framework/tactic in full (our exhibits: five-blank sentence, mom-test swaps) | **no** |
| | **Opinion / contrarian** | a belief stated flat that sparks debate ("Jira is not progress") | **no** |
| | **Story / lesson** | a real thing that happened + the takeaway (incl. mistakes) | **no** |
| **20% personal** | **Build-in-public** | real audit findings, what Paul's seeing across founders, changed-my-mind | rarely |
| | **Curation / reaction** | Paul's take on an industry thing (an AI tool, a thread, a study) - NEW, we do none | no |
| **10% promo** | **Course / validation** | the ask - but framed as story/lesson | **yes (first comment)** |
| (weekly) | **Poll** | engagement slot, tests a topic | lane-dependent |

**Two behavioural changes that matter most:**
1. **Decouple value from the funnel.** ~10% of posts get a course link; the rest
   give value freely. The free 70% is what makes the 10% land.
2. **Add the two pillars we do zero of:** *curation/reaction* (cheapest "interesting")
   and *build-in-public* (real audit findings, Paul's POV). These need no course tie.

## What this changes operationally

- `content-plan.md` gains a **pillar column** and a per-window ratio check (not
  just lane rotation).
- Most existing drafts are already "teach/opinion/story" - we mostly need to
  **drop the reflexive course link** from the 70% and *add* curation + build-in-public.
- Consider carousels for multi-point exhibits; keep polls weekly.
- Engage 10-15 min before posting, reply in the first 2 hours (algorithm + it's
  how Holub/Cutler actually grow).
