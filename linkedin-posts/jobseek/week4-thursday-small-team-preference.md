I want to run an engineering team of up to fifteen engineers. Twenty-five is the outside edge, and above that I am doing a different job than the one I want.

Size is the smaller half of it. The seat has to report to a founder or a director I work with as a partner. Where it reports into an existing layer of engineering managers, the decisions I would be hired to make are already owned by a committee, and the headcount underneath does not change that. Leads reporting to me are fine. A layer sitting above me is the problem.

The team I want is flat and senior enough that nobody needs managing, with the feedback arriving from the work instead of from me. Basecamp-shaped. No ceremony that exists so someone can report upward.

Plenty of good engineering happens at much larger scale. I am saying which side of it I want to be on, and where I am actually useful.

For engineering leaders who took a seat one layer removed from the founder: how long before you noticed, and what was the first thing you lost?
