If an AI wrote most of your product, who do you ask why it works the way it does?

With a cheap developer you at least had a person who made the choices. You could ask what happens when a payment fails and hear whether the answer was solid or invented. AI takes that person away. It answers with the same confidence whether it's right or not.

The check I use: pick one screen that touches money or user data. Ask whoever is building it to explain, in plain words, what happens when two people use it at once, and when someone sends bad input. Not what the code says. What happens.

If the answer is "the AI handled that", or they come back with more AI output, nobody senior has read this.

What do you do when nobody on your side can check the answer?
