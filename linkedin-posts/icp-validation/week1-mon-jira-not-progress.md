A lot of non-technical founders read a full Jira board as proof the product is moving.

It looks fine. Tickets are closing, standups are clean, the team sounds busy.

But a closed ticket just means someone worked on it. It doesn't mean there's anything you can open and use. A dev shop can finish a whole sprint and still not have built one usable thing.

The check I'd run: don't ask how the sprint went. Ask for one link where this week's work is live. Open it yourself and try to do the thing they said they shipped - place the order, send the message, whatever it was. If you can't do it in a few minutes, it isn't done yet.

This works even if you can't read a line of code. You're just using the product like a customer would.

If you're non-technical and working with a dev shop, how do you check what actually shipped?
