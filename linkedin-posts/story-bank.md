## Convictions / POV (Paul's flat beliefs - the opinion pillar)

- Don't hire a dev team before you've validated demand; no-code/AI is enough to
  prove the idea first.
- Vibe coding belongs at the FRONT (prototype, validate, throwaway), engineering
  discipline at the BACK (payments, user data, production). [matches the 2026
  vibe-coding debate consensus - see trends.md]
- "Would you use this?" buys a polite yes; ask what they already DID about the
  problem. (Mom Test)
- The cheapest test of an idea is a real price in front of a stranger, not a
  compliment from a friend.
- AI now out-builds a big share of the freelancers a non-technical founder would
  hire - IF they know how to drive it. (soft; the hard % is a future research post)
