"Everyone I show it to says they'd love it."

A founder said that to me on a call in June. Eleven people had seen her prototype by then, all of them enthusiastic. So I asked how many had used it since. She went quiet for a second, then checked. None.

Nobody in that eleven was lying to hurt her. They were friends, and trashing a friend's idea over coffee feels mean, so people don't. The kinder someone is to you, the less their "I'd totally use that" tells you.

She's redoing the conversations this month with one change: instead of showing the app and asking "would you use this," she's asking strangers who have the problem to walk her through the last time it actually cost them something. What someone did last Tuesday is the only answer they can't be polite about.

Anyone else collected a pile of compliments that never turned into a single user?
