Frequently asked questions

I can't tell if the code is any good.

You shouldn't have to. A Sprint ends with one feature live for your users. Judge that, not the code. Our clients rate us 4.8/5 on Clutch. A staging link in week one, a plain-English note every Friday and one mid-sprint call.

Will you disappear halfway through?

~95% of our clients stay with us. Mobile Coach worked with us for 9+ years, Agent Inbox for 50+ months.

Who are the engineers?

They average 8+ years of experience each. Every case on this site is a Ruby on Rails product. Agent Inbox also runs React and Vue front ends.

Will we end up dependent on you?

Our clients' own teams grew while we were there: Agent Inbox from 1 to 6 developers, Open Apply from 2 to 20. When you hire, we pair with your new engineers and ramp down on two weeks' notice.

What happens if my engineer leaves?

If an engineer leaves or is not working out for you, we name a replacement within 5 working days and pay for the handover.

Who owns the code?

Yours from the first commit, IP included.

Will you sign an NDA?

Mutual NDA on request, signed before we read your code.

What does it cost?

Sprint: $3,200. Embedded: $1,600 per engineer per week ($50 an hour, up to 32 hours), no minimum term, two weeks' notice.

How soon can you start?

Work starts on a dated Monday, usually within two weeks of signing.
